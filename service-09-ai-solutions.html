Process

How we work

Every project follows the same clear, structured process.

01 Discovery

Understanding the client's business, goals and requirements in detail before any design or development begins.

02 Planning & Design

Creating a wireframe or structural plan and sharing it with the client for approval before development starts.

03 Development

Building the solution in stages, with regular progress updates shared with the client throughout.

04 Testing

Thorough testing across devices and use cases to ensure everything works as expected before delivery.

05 Delivery & Support

Final delivery of the completed product, followed by ongoing support and maintenance as needed.
