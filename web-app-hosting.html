Overview

About Web & App Hosting

We set up and manage the hosting your website or app runs on: domain, DNS, SSL certificate, deployment, backups and updates, so it stays online and secure.

Choose a hosting plan and order it online. We register or connect your domain, deploy your site, and look after it, with optional monthly care through WAMS.

Best for

  • Businesses launching a new website or store
  • Owners whose current host is slow or hard to manage
  • Teams that want one contact for domain, hosting and email
  • Anyone who wants their site moved without downtime worries

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to own a domain already?

No. We can register a domain for you or connect one you already own.

Can you move my existing website?

Yes. Tell us where it is hosted today and we plan the move so your site stays available.

How are hosting plans priced?

Plans and prices are listed on this page when available, or quoted after a short consultation based on what you host.

What happens if my site goes down?

We monitor your site and act when it does. Optional WAMS plans add regular maintenance and reports.

What is needed to get started?

A completed requirement form and an initial advance payment to begin work. The form on this site asks the questions that matter for this service.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes. Optional monthly maintenance and support plans are available after delivery.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Project details and materials you share are treated as confidential and not shared with third parties without consent. We can sign an NDA on request.
