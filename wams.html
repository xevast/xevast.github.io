Overview

About WAMS

WAMS is Web and App Management Service. It is ongoing care after launch, so your website or app stays updated, monitored, secured and working.

Choose a monthly plan and we handle monitoring, backups, patches, fixes and reporting, even for sites we did not build.

Best for

  • Owners who have no one to call when the site breaks
  • Businesses with an app in the stores that needs steady releases
  • Teams that worry about updates, backups and security
  • Anyone who wants a monthly report instead of surprises

FAQ

Common questions

Can you manage a site you did not build?

Yes. We start with an audit and document what we find.

Which technologies?

WordPress, custom PHP/Node, React and mobile apps. Tell us your stack and we will confirm.

What if my site is hacked?

We isolate, clean, restore from backup and harden it. Premium gets the fastest response.

Can I cancel?

Plans are monthly. Terms are confirmed in your quote.

What is needed to get started?

A completed requirement form and an initial advance payment to begin work. The form on this site asks the questions that matter for this service.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Project details and materials you share are treated as confidential and not shared with third parties without consent. We can sign an NDA on request.
