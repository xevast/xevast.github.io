FAQ

Common questions

Who can join the Lab?

Students, working professionals and teams. Each programme lists its level and requirements.

Is there a fee?

Fees and dates are announced with each programme. Register your interest and we will tell you first.

Do I need an account?

No. You can register your interest without an account.

How is the Lab different from the Academy?

The Academy teaches structured courses. The Lab is for practice, projects and research that build on what you learn.
