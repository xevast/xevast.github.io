FAQ

Common questions

Do we have to stop work while you digitize?

No. We move in stages and keep your current process running until each new system is tested and your staff are trained.

What happens to our old data?

We digitise and clean it, then import it into the new systems. You also receive a copy of the cleaned data.

Why is the price confirmed after an audit?

Every business starts from a different place. The audit shows what is already digital, what is not and what to do first, so the quote matches the real work.

Can our staff use it without training?

We include staff training, video tutorials and SOP documents so your team can use the systems from day one.
