01 · About Xevast

Xevast is a technology company founded with a single purpose: to help businesses grow through well-built, reliable, and modern technology. The company was created to bring together core digital services — web and app development, software development, cloud technology, and cybersecurity — under one dependable brand, rather than businesses having to look for separate vendors for each need.

The name Xevast and its mark — two angular blades in red and white forming an 'X' inside a circular ring — were designed to represent speed, precision, and duality: the balance between innovation (red) and reliability (white), working together toward one outcome.

Origin

Xevast was founded by Anshu Kumar, who serves as Founder and CEO. The company began as a single-founder initiative, with the intention of building it into a full-fledged technology company — and eventually, a parent company overseeing multiple specialized divisions as the business grows.

Mission

To empower businesses through technology, innovation, and excellence — turning digital ideas into reliable, real-world solutions.

Vision

To grow from a single technology company into a parent company that nurtures multiple specialized businesses — each carrying forward the same standard of trust and quality Xevast was built on.

Core Values

  • Innovation — using the right technology for each problem, not just the familiar one.
  • Reliability — delivering work that is stable, secure, and built to last.
  • Transparency — clear communication with clients at every stage of a project.
  • Client-first thinking — solutions shaped around the client's business, not a fixed template.

10 · Business Structure & Growth Plan

Xevast is currently structured as a single company offering all services under one brand. The long-term plan is for Xevast to function as a parent company, with each major service area eventually operating as its own dedicated entity.

14 · Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical website take to build?

Timelines depend on scope, but most standard business websites are completed within two to three weeks of finalized requirements.

Do you provide support after the project is delivered?

Yes. Optional ongoing maintenance and support arrangements are available after delivery.

Who owns the final code and design?

Full ownership of the completed work transfers to the client upon final payment.

What is needed to get started on a project?

A completed requirement form and an initial advance payment to begin work.

Can Xevast improve or redesign an existing website or system?

Yes — redesigning and modernizing existing websites, software, and infrastructure is a core part of what Xevast offers.

Does Xevast offer security testing?

Yes. Ethical hacking and VAPT services identify and help fix vulnerabilities in a client's systems through authorized testing.

Can you build a mobile app as well as a website?

Yes. Mobile app development is offered as its own division, alongside web development and UI/UX design.
