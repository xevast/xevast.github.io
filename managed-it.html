Overview

About Managed IT

We give you ongoing care so that systems stay updated, monitored and working: monthly maintenance and support, server and system monitoring, and a technical helpdesk.

Your team gets a point of contact when something needs attention.

Best for

  • Offices without an in-house IT person
  • Businesses with laptops, email and shared files to keep running
  • Teams that need reliable backups
  • Owners who want one contact for all technical issues

FAQ

Common questions

Do you support on-site?

Scope is agreed in your plan; remote support is the default.

How do I raise a request?

Through the Support page or WhatsApp.

What is needed to get started?

A completed requirement form and an initial advance payment to begin work. The form on this site asks the questions that matter for this service.

Do you provide support after delivery?

Yes. Optional monthly maintenance and support plans are available after delivery.

Yes. Project details and materials you share are treated as confidential and not shared with third parties without consent. We can sign an NDA on request.
