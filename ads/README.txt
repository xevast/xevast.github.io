XEVAST BILLBOARD (home page)

The billboard plays automatically: one intro slide, then every service as an ad slide (names, descriptions and links come from js/site-data.js). Add a service there and it appears here.
To add your own ad PHOTOS (no video):
1. Create the folder ads/images/ and copy the photo in (jpg, png, webp, 16:9, under 8 MB).
2. Write its name in ads/ads.js inside XEVAST_ADS_FILES, e.g. "ad1.jpg". Save and refresh.
Settings in ads/ads.js: XEVAST_ADS_BRAND (intro + services), XEVAST_ADS_SERVICES (service slides only).
