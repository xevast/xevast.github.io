FAQ

Common questions

Is this enough for a new business?

It is enough to look professional and be found online. When you need payments, tools and marketing, you can upgrade to the New Business Launchpad.

Will I lose anything when I upgrade?

No. The brand, website and accounts are reused, so you pay only for the extra work.

Is it only for new businesses?

No. Running businesses also use it to refresh their brand and online presence before a larger digitization.

How long does it take?

Usually one to two weeks after you approve the brand direction.
