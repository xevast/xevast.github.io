FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a registered company before I start?

No. We can start with your brand and website while you decide on registrations. GST, Udyam and similar registrations are handled with trusted CA partners when you are ready.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. We build in stages, and everything built in one stage is reused in the next. Digital Identity Setup is a lighter way to begin.

Who owns the website, domain and accounts?

Full ownership of the completed work, accounts and files transfers to you on final payment, along with a document listing every login.

How long does a launch take?

Most launches take three to five weeks after requirements are final. Larger scopes are planned in stages.
