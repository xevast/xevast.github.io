FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How long does a typical website take?

Timelines depend on scope, but most standard business websites are completed within two to three weeks of finalized requirements.

Do you provide support after the project is delivered?

Yes. Optional ongoing maintenance and support arrangements are available after delivery.

Who owns the final code and design?

Full ownership of the completed work transfers to you upon final payment.

What is needed to get started?

A completed requirement form and an initial advance payment (typically 30–50%) to begin work.

Yes — redesigning and modernizing existing websites, software and infrastructure is a core part of what we offer.
